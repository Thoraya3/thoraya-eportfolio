# Numerical Analysis – Unit 11 Reflection

<br>

## What? (Description)

In Unit 11 of Numerical Analysis, I studied regression analysis and its role in statistical modelling and prediction. The unit focused on understanding how regression models estimate relationships between dependent and independent variables and how these models can be used to explain and predict outcomes.

<br>

The unit built directly upon Unit 10, which introduced correlation analysis. While correlation measures the strength and direction of association between variables, regression analysis provides an equation that can be used to predict one variable from another.

<br>

I learned about simple linear regression, multiple regression, residuals, least squares estimation, regression assumptions, model fit statistics, ANOVA tables, and the interpretation of regression coefficients.

<br>

The Marketing dataset was used to demonstrate how sales can be predicted from advertising expenditure. Through this example, I learned how to create scatter plots, calculate correlation coefficients, fit regression models in R, interpret regression equations, and evaluate statistical significance.

<br>

I also completed Data Activity 9 using the Health_Data dataset. The activity required me to perform simple linear regression analysis to predict diastolic blood pressure from systolic blood pressure and interpret the findings at the 5% significance level.

<br>

In addition, Unit 11 included the completion of the Summary Post for Collaborative Discussion 2. This allowed me to reflect on my original discussion, evaluate feedback from peers, and develop a deeper understanding of confidence intervals, p-values, and statistical interpretation.

<br>

## So What? (Interpretation)

Unit 11 demonstrated how regression analysis extends beyond describing relationships and allows predictions to be made using statistical models. This represents an important transition from descriptive and inferential statistics towards predictive analytics and modelling.

<br>

One of the most valuable concepts introduced was the distinction between correlation and regression. Correlation simply measures association, whereas regression introduces direction and prediction. The unit reinforced that correlation does not imply causation, while regression attempts to model the influence of independent variables on a dependent variable.

<br>

The Marketing dataset example helped me understand how regression models can be used to explain sales based on advertising expenditure. Learning how to interpret regression coefficients, residuals, p-values, and adjusted R² values strengthened my ability to evaluate the practical usefulness of statistical models.

<br>

As a Senior Application Programmer in the telecommunications industry, regression analysis has direct relevance to my professional responsibilities. Similar techniques can be applied to predict service demand, estimate system performance, analyse automation outcomes, forecast operational trends, and support business planning.

<br>

The Summary Post activity also reinforced the importance of critical statistical interpretation. Through discussion and peer feedback, I learned that statistical significance alone should not drive decisions. Confidence intervals, effect sizes, data quality, and practical context must also be considered.

<br>

Overall, the unit helped me connect statistical analysis with predictive modelling and provided a strong foundation for future work in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.
